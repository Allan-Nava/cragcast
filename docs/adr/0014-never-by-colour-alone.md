# 0014 — The verdict is never told by colour alone

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Backlog:** CG-21, CG-23, CG-55, CG-51

## Context

Green, yellow and red are the obvious verdict colours, and about one man in twelve cannot tell
red from green.

## Decision

Every verdict has a shape, a letter and words: circle S "si va", diamond F "forse", square N
"no", with at least 4.5:1 contrast for the letter. Cluster marks are neutral and write the split
out. The axe scan in CI fails on serious or critical findings.

## Consequences

- Marks are slightly busier than dots.
- New verdict surfaces follow the same rule, checked by the end-to-end accessibility scan.
